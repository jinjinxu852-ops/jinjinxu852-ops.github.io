Frequently asked questions

What information do you need to quote a custom peptide?

The amino acid sequence from N- to C-terminus, any modifications, the quantity in milligrams and the purity you need. If you are unsure about purity, describe the application and we will recommend one.

How long does a custom peptide take?

Standard sequences typically ship within 2–3 weeks. Long, hydrophobic, cyclic or heavily modified peptides take longer. Every quote states the expected delivery time for that specific peptide.

Which purity should I order?

It depends on the experiment. Antibody production and screening often work at >75% to >85%, while cell assays, binding studies and in vivo work usually need >95% or >98%. Our peptide purity guide lists common applications.

How is peptide purity measured?

By analytical reversed-phase HPLC with UV detection at 220 nm. Purity is the main peak area as a percentage of total peak area. Identity is confirmed separately by mass spectrometry.

Why is the amount of peptide less than the weight of the powder?

The powder contains peptide plus bound water plus counter-ions, typically trifluoroacetate. Net peptide content is usually 50% to 90%. If you need an accurate concentration, order net peptide content analysis and base your calculation on that figure.

What salt form will my peptide be in?

TFA salt by default, because trifluoroacetic acid is used during cleavage and purification. Acetate and hydrochloride forms are available on request and are recommended for cell culture and in vivo work.

Can you make modified peptides?

Yes. Acetylation, amidation, biotin, fluorescent dyes, stable isotopes, phosphorylation, cyclisation, D-amino acids, N-methylation and reactive warheads such as CMK and FMK are all available, and can be combined on one peptide.

Do you make peptide libraries?

Yes. Overlapping, alanine scanning, truncation and positional scanning libraries, delivered in tubes or 96-well plates, crude or purified.

What is the longest peptide you can synthesise?

Up to 160 residues as a routine service. Longer targets may be possible through a fragment condensation approach; send the sequence and we will assess it before you commit.

Can you make a peptide that another supplier failed to deliver?

Often yes. Send the sequence and tell us what happened. Difficult sequences usually need a different strategy rather than a second attempt at the same one.

Will you keep my sequence confidential?

Yes. All customer sequences are treated as confidential and we are happy to sign an NDA before you share project details.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, worldwide. Peptides ship as lyophilised powder at ambient temperature, which is stable in transit. Contact us for options and lead times for your country.

What documents come with the peptide?

An analytical HPLC chromatogram, a mass spectrum and a certificate of analysis stating the sequence, quantity, purity, salt form and lot number.

My peptide will not dissolve. What should I do?

Check the net charge of the sequence. Positively charged peptides dissolve in water, sometimes helped by a trace of dilute acetic acid; negatively charged ones by a trace of base. Hydrophobic or near-neutral peptides usually need a small volume of DMSO or DMF first, then slow dilution into buffer. Our reconstitution procedure explains the full approach.

How should I store my peptide?

Lyophilised at -20°C, or -80°C for the long term, always warmed to room temperature before opening so moisture does not condense onto it. In solution, aliquot once and freeze, and never refreeze a thawed aliquot.

My peptide worked before and now it does not. Why?

Most often moisture uptake from repeated opening of a cold vial, oxidation of methionine or cysteine in solution, or aggregation after several freeze-thaw cycles. An HPLC and MS check will show which.

Can you analyse a peptide you did not make?

Yes. We offer HPLC, LC-MS, MALDI-TOF MS, net peptide content and counter-ion analysis on samples from any source.

How do I place a repeat order?

Give us the previous lot number and we will reproduce the same specification. Repeat orders are usually faster, since the method is already developed.
