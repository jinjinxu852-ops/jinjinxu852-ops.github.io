Custom Peptide Library Synthesis

Tens to hundreds of related peptides, made in parallel and delivered in the format your screen needs. Tell us the parent sequence and the type of library, and we design the rest.

Request a Quote See price list

Library size

48 peptides minimum, up to a full 96-well plate.

Purity options

Crude with MS check, or purified to >70%, >85% or >95%.

Delivery format

Individual tubes or 96-well plates, lyophilised.

Turnaround

Typically 2–3 weeks, depending on size and purity.

Types of peptide library we make

Overlapping libraries. Peptides of fixed length that step along the parent protein sequence with a defined offset, for example 15-mers overlapping by 11 residues. Used to locate linear B-cell and T-cell epitopes.

Alanine scanning libraries. Each residue of the parent peptide is replaced by alanine in turn, showing which positions are essential for activity or binding.

Truncation libraries. Residues are removed from the N- or C-terminus step by step, to find the shortest sequence that keeps activity.

Positional scanning and substitution libraries. One position is varied across a set of amino acids, to map tolerance at that site during lead optimisation.

Custom lists. If you already have the sequences, send the list and we will quote it as it stands.

What we need from you

  • The parent sequence, or a list of sequences
  • Library type, peptide length and offset
  • Quantity per peptide and purity level
  • Any modification that applies to the whole library, such as N-terminal acetylation, C-terminal amidation or biotinylation
  • Delivery format: tubes or 96-well plates

Quality control

Every peptide in a crude library is checked by mass spectrometry to confirm the expected molecular weight. Purified libraries also receive analytical HPLC. Data is supplied as one report covering the whole library, so you can see the status of each well at a glance. See quality and process control for the full procedure.

Working with the library after delivery

Libraries arrive lyophilised. Dissolve each peptide according to its own properties, not a single protocol for the plate: hydrophobic sequences often need a small amount of organic solvent first. Our reconstitution procedure and handling and storage pages explain how to prepare and store stocks so the library survives repeated use.

After the screen

Hits from a library screen are usually resynthesised at larger scale and higher purity for follow-up work. We can do this as custom peptide synthesis, and add modifications such as fluorescent labels for binding assays.

Frequently asked questions

What is a peptide library used for?

Peptide libraries are used for epitope mapping, T-cell assays, protein-protein interaction studies, kinase substrate profiling and structure-activity relationship studies during lead optimisation.

Should I order a crude or a purified peptide library?

Crude libraries are cost-effective for first-pass binding screens such as ELISA epitope mapping. Cell-based and quantitative assays usually need purified peptides, because truncated sequences and residual reagents in crude material can affect the result.

How much of each peptide will I receive?

Each library peptide is supplied at 3–5 mg. Larger amounts of selected hits can be resynthesised at higher purity.
