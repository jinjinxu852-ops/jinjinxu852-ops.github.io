What determines the price of a custom peptide

Length. Each additional amino acid is another coupling cycle with its own reagents and time. Price rises roughly in step with the number of residues, and more steeply once a sequence passes about 30 residues, because yield falls as the chain grows.

Quantity. Larger scale means more resin and more reagent, but the fixed costs of method development, purification and QC are spread over more material. Cost per milligram therefore falls as the order grows.

Purity. Reaching >95% or >98% requires more preparative HPLC runs and discards more material. Ordering a higher purity than your experiment needs is the most common way to overpay. Our peptide purity guide matches purity levels to applications.

Modifications. Each label, cyclisation or unusual residue adds a building block and often extra steps. See peptide modifications for what is available.

Sequence difficulty. Two 20-mers can cost very differently. Hydrophobic stretches, several cysteines and aspartimide-prone motifs all need extra work, which is why we review the sequence before quoting.

How to get an exact price

Published tables cannot cover every sequence. Send the sequence, quantity, purity and any modifications through the quote request form and you will receive a firm price and delivery time. If your budget is fixed, say so: we can often suggest a purity or scale that fits it.

Ways to reduce cost

  • Order the purity your assay needs, not the highest available
  • Order a realistic quantity; peptides degrade in storage, so a huge batch is not always a saving
  • Group related peptides into one library order instead of ordering them one by one
  • Screen with crude material first, then resynthesise only the hits at high purity
  • Tell us about future repeat orders, so the method developed now can be reused

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom peptide cost?

A short unmodified research peptide at moderate purity is the least expensive case; price rises with length, quantity, purity and each modification. Because these factors interact, we quote each sequence individually rather than publishing a fixed per-residue rate.

Why do two peptides of the same length cost different amounts?

Difficult sequences need more coupling steps, more reagent and more purification to reach the same purity. Hydrophobic stretches, multiple cysteines and aggregation-prone motifs all add work.

Do you offer discounts for larger orders?

Yes. Cost per milligram falls as scale increases, and libraries and repeat orders of the same sequence are quoted at a lower rate. Tell us your expected total volume when you ask for a quote.

Is there a charge if the synthesis fails?

Our quoting terms for difficult sequences are stated in each quote. Contact us at info@chempeptide.com for the current policy.
