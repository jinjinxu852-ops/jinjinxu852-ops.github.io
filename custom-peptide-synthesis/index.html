What custom peptide synthesis covers

Custom peptide synthesis means a peptide made to order for one customer: your sequence, your quantity, your purity and your modifications. It is the right choice when the peptide you need is not available from a catalogue, or when you need a specific salt form, label or batch size.

ChemPeptide has made custom peptides in Shanghai for more than ten years. Each project is handled by a chemist who reviews the sequence before synthesis starts, so problems such as aggregation, aspartimide formation or oxidation-prone residues are planned for and not discovered afterwards.

How we make your peptide

  1. Sequence review. We assess the sequence for difficult regions and agree purity, salt form and modifications with you.
  2. Solid-phase synthesis. The chain is assembled on resin using Fmoc chemistry. Read more about our peptide synthesis technologies.
  3. Cleavage and purification. The crude peptide is cleaved from the resin and purified by preparative reversed-phase HPLC.
  4. Quality control. Purity is confirmed by analytical HPLC and identity by mass spectrometry. See quality and process control.
  5. Lyophilisation and shipping. The peptide is freeze-dried, aliquoted if requested, and shipped with its data.

Choosing a purity level

Higher purity costs more and takes longer, so it pays to order what the experiment needs and no more. As a rule of thumb:

  • Crude to >70%: initial screening, peptide arrays.
  • >75% to >85%: polyclonal antibody production, epitope mapping, enzyme substrate screening.
  • >90% to >95%: in vitro bioassays, quantitative receptor-ligand studies, monoclonal antibody production.
  • >98%: in vivo studies, NMR, crystallography, clinical-trial-enabling research.

Our recommended peptide purity guide lists more applications.

Modifications available

Most custom peptides carry at least one modification. Common requests include N-terminal acetylation and C-terminal amidation, biotin, fluorescent labels, stable isotope labels, phosphorylation and cyclisation. The full list is on our peptide modifications page.

Frequently asked questions

What information do you need to quote a custom peptide?

The amino acid sequence (N- to C-terminus), any modifications, the quantity in mg, and the purity you need. If you are unsure about purity, tell us the application and we will recommend one.

How long does custom peptide synthesis take?

Standard sequences typically ship within 2–3 weeks. Long, hydrophobic or heavily modified peptides take longer, and we state the expected delivery time in every quote.

Which purity should I order?

It depends on the experiment. Antibody production and screening often work with >70% to >85%, while quantitative assays, NMR and in vivo studies usually need >95% or >98%. See our peptide purity guide for details.

In what form will I receive the peptide?

As a lyophilised powder, by default as the TFA salt. Acetate or hydrochloride salt forms are available on request.
