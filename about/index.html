About ChemPeptide

Chempeptide Limited has supplied high quality peptides to life science researchers worldwide since 2006. From our Shanghai laboratory we deliver thousands of custom peptides every year to research institutions across the world.

What we do

ChemPeptide makes peptides to order, from 2 to 160 amino acids and from milligram to kilogram scale. That covers short research peptides for a single experiment, libraries of up to a full 96-well plate for screening, and heavily modified peptides carrying labels, bridges or reactive groups. We also hold a range of catalog peptides in stock for immediate delivery, and run analytical testing on our own peptides and on samples made elsewhere.

Our staff has decades of experience in the design, synthesis and production of peptides using Fmoc solid phase and solution phase technology. Everything is done by one team in one laboratory, so the chemist who reviews your sequence is part of the same group that purifies and releases the finished material.

What we deliver

  • Custom synthesis from 2 to 160 amino acids in length
  • Milligram to kilogram quantities
  • Purity grades from desalted through >70%, >80%, >85%, >90%, >95%, >98% and above 99%
  • Free N-terminal acetylation and C-terminal amidation
  • Synthesis success rate above 98%
  • Mass spectrometry and RP-HPLC data with every peptide
  • Typical turnaround of 2 to 3 weeks, with crude peptides often within a week

How we work

A chemist reviews every sequence before it is quoted. Peptides fail for predictable reasons — aggregation on the resin, aspartimide formation, uncontrolled disulfide pairing — and the point of the review is to plan for them rather than to discover them halfway through a synthesis.

When a sequence turns out to be harder than expected, we tell the customer and discuss the options. We would rather have that conversation than deliver something that quietly falls short of the agreed specification.

Who we work with

  • Academic laboratories needing reliable research peptides at a sensible price
  • Biotech and pharmaceutical R&D teams in screening and lead optimisation
  • Diagnostic and antibody companies needing immunogens, epitope libraries and labelled standards
  • Analytical laboratories needing reference peptides and internal standards

Confidentiality

Customer sequences are confidential. We do not disclose them, reuse them or add them to a catalogue, and we are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share project details.

Contact

Technical questions go to our technical support team, and there is no charge for advice on a sequence or a specification. For prices and delivery times, use the quote request form, or see our contact details.
