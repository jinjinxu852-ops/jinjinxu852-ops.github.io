Peptide Analysis Services

Analytical testing on our peptides or on yours. Send a sample and tell us the question — what is it, how pure is it, how much peptide is actually in the vial — and we will answer it with data.

The four questions analysis answers

Is it the right peptide? Mass spectrometry compares the measured molecular weight with the value calculated from the sequence. A match confirms identity; a difference often points to a specific problem, such as a missing residue, an oxidised methionine or an incomplete deprotection.

How pure is it? Reversed-phase HPLC separates the target peptide from related impurities — truncated and deletion sequences, oxidation products, partially modified species — and reports the main peak as a percentage of total peak area.

How much peptide is in the vial? The powder you weigh is peptide plus water plus counter-ions. Net peptide content by amino acid analysis gives the true peptide fraction, which can be anywhere from about 50% to 90% of the weight.

What else is in there? TFA and acetate content quantifies the counter-ions left from synthesis and purification, which matters for cell culture and in vivo work.

Services

  • Routine HPLC and MS analysis: the standard purity and identity package, included with every peptide we make.
  • LC-MS analysis: separation and mass detection in one run, for complex mixtures and modified peptides.
  • MALDI-TOF MS: fast, tolerant molecular weight confirmation, well suited to long peptides.
  • Peptide content (elemental analysis): net peptide content for accurate concentrations.
  • TFA and acetate content analysis: counter-ion quantitation and salt exchange.

Sending a sample

Include the sequence, the expected molecular weight, the amount supplied and anything you already know about the material. If the peptide came from elsewhere and is behaving unexpectedly in your assay, describe the symptom — it tells us which test is most likely to find the cause.

Contact us at info@chempeptide.com or through the quote request form before shipping, so we can confirm the test panel and give you the shipping address and conditions.

What you receive

A report containing the chromatograms and spectra, the numerical results, the method used and the conditions. Data is supplied in a form you can include in a thesis, a publication or a regulatory submission.

Frequently asked questions

Can you analyse a peptide that was not made by ChemPeptide?

Yes. We accept samples from other sources for identity, purity and content testing. Send the sequence, the expected molecular weight and any known history of the sample, so we can interpret what we find.

Which analysis do I actually need?

For routine use, HPLC purity and a mass check are enough. If you need an exact concentration, add net peptide content. If an assay is sensitive to trifluoroacetate, add counter-ion analysis. For complex or multiply modified peptides, LC-MS separates species a single HPLC peak may hide.

How much sample do you need?

Typically 1–2 mg for a standard panel. Tell us how much you can spare and we will say what is possible with that amount.

What is the turnaround for analysis only?

Usually 1–2 weeks from receipt of the sample. Contact us for urgent requests.
