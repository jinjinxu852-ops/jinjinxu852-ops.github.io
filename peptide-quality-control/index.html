Quality & Process Control

A peptide is only useful if you can trust what is in the vial. Every order passes the same documented checks, from the sequence review before synthesis starts to the data that ships with the powder.

Chromatogram, mass spectrum and certificate of analysis.

Control starts before synthesis

Most peptide failures can be predicted from the sequence. Before a project is accepted, a chemist reviews it for the motifs that cause trouble: long hydrophobic stretches that aggregate on the resin, Asp-Gly sequences prone to aspartimide formation, multiple cysteines that need a controlled oxidation strategy, and N-terminal glutamine that can cyclise.

The review decides the synthesis route, the resin, the cleavage cocktail and the realistic purity target. It also tells us when to say that a target is difficult, so you are not surprised later.

In-process checks

During chain assembly we monitor whether the couplings are completing. If a step stalls, it is repeated or the conditions are adjusted before the next residue is added — a problem caught at residue 12 is fixable, while the same problem found at the end is not.

After cleavage, the crude peptide is analysed before purification. This tells us the quality of the crude material and guides the purification method.

Release testing

Every peptide is tested before it ships:

  • Analytical HPLC for purity, by reversed-phase separation with UV detection at 220 nm
  • Mass spectrometry for identity, confirming the observed molecular weight matches the theoretical value

Peptides are released only when both results meet the agreed specification. Additional tests are available through our peptide analysis services, including net peptide content, counter-ion content and LC-MS.

Traceability

Each batch carries a lot number that links the delivered material to its synthesis record and analytical data. If you order the same peptide again, tell us the previous lot number and we will reproduce the same specification.

What we do not claim

Our Shanghai facility is a research peptide laboratory. Regulatory status, certifications and audit history are available on request; if you need material produced under a particular standard, tell us before ordering so we can confirm what is possible.

Your part in keeping the quality

Peptides can degrade after delivery through moisture, repeated freeze-thaw cycles or oxidation in solution. Our handling and storage and reconstitution procedure pages explain how to keep the material in the state it arrived in.

Frequently asked questions

What documents come with my peptide?

An analytical HPLC chromatogram showing purity, a mass spectrum confirming the molecular weight, and a certificate of analysis listing the sequence, quantity, purity, salt form and lot number.

What does the stated purity actually mean?

It is the percentage of the main peak area in the analytical HPLC trace, measured by UV absorbance at 220 nm. It describes purity relative to other peptide-related species; it does not include water, salts or counter-ions, which is what net peptide content measures.

What happens if the peptide does not meet specification?

We do not ship it. If a batch fails release testing we repurify or resynthesise it. If a delivered peptide does not match its certificate of analysis, contact us and we will investigate the lot.
