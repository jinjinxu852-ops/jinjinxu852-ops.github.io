How to Order

Five steps from a sequence in an email to a vial on your bench. Here is what happens at each one, and what we need from you to keep it moving.

Request a Quote See price list

1. Send the sequence

Sequence, quantity, purity and any modifications.

2. Receive a quote

Firm price and delivery time, usually within 24 hours.

3. Confirm the order

Purchase order or written confirmation starts the work.

4. Synthesis and QC

We make it, test it and release it against spec.

Step 1 — Send us the specification

Use the quote request form or email info@chempeptide.com with:

  • The sequence, written N-terminus to C-terminus, with modifications included — for example Ac-SGRGK(biotin)GGKGLGKGGA-NH2
  • The quantity in milligrams
  • The purity grade, or the application if you would like us to recommend one
  • The salt form, if your assay is sensitive to trifluoroacetate
  • Your deadline, if you have one

If you are unsure how to express a modification, describe it in words. Our chemists will write the specification and confirm it with you.

Step 2 — Review and quote

A chemist reviews the sequence for difficult motifs before quoting, which is why a quote sometimes takes a little longer than an automated price would. You receive:

  • A firm price for the specification as written
  • The expected delivery time for that particular sequence
  • Any warnings about the sequence, and alternatives worth considering

If the specification looks more expensive than it needs to be — a higher purity than the application requires, for instance — we will say so.

Step 3 — Confirm the order

Send a purchase order, or confirm the quote by email if your organisation does not require one. Include:

  • The quote reference
  • Billing and delivery addresses
  • Any internal reference you want to appear on the invoice
  • The name and email of the person to contact with technical questions

Confidentiality agreements, if needed, are signed at this point or earlier. Payment terms are set out on this page.

Step 4 — Synthesis and quality control

Work begins once the order is confirmed. The peptide is assembled, cleaved, purified and tested as described under quality and process control. If the sequence behaves unexpectedly, we contact you rather than silently delivering something different from the agreed specification.

Step 5 — Delivery

Your order arrives as lyophilised powder with:

  • Analytical HPLC chromatogram
  • Mass spectrum
  • Certificate of analysis with sequence, quantity, purity, salt form and lot number

Transfer it to the freezer on arrival and read the handling and storage guidance before use. The reconstitution procedure explains how to dissolve it without losing material.

Repeat orders

Quote the previous lot number and we will reproduce the same specification. Repeat orders are usually faster, because the synthesis method is already developed, and large or recurring volumes are quoted at a lower rate — tell us if you expect to order regularly.

Get a quote

Send your sequence, quantity and purity. A peptide chemist will reply with price and lead time.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a purchase order?

A purchase order is the usual way institutional customers confirm an order, but a written email confirmation of the quote is also sufficient. Tell us which your organisation requires.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms, accepted methods and currency are set out below. For first orders from new customers we may ask for payment in advance; established accounts are invoiced on net 30 terms.

Can I change the specification after ordering?

Before synthesis begins, usually yes — contact us as soon as possible. Once the chain is being assembled, changes to sequence or modification mean starting again, which is charged as a new order.

How is the peptide shipped?

As lyophilised powder at ambient temperature, which is stable in transit. We use FedEx or DHL for international shipments and provide tracking. Customs documentation is included.
