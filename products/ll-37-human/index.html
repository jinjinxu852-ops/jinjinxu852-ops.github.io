LL-37 (Human)

LL-37 is the only human cathelicidin-derived antimicrobial peptide. In stock, shipped as lyophilised powder with HPLC and MS data.

CAS number 154947-66-7
Purity >95% or >98%

Description

LL-37 is the only human cathelicidin-derived antimicrobial peptide. The 37-residue amphipathic helix disrupts bacterial membranes and also acts as an immune signalling molecule, with roles in chemotaxis, wound healing and angiogenesis, which has made it a target of interest well beyond infection research.

Applications

  • Antimicrobial and antibiofilm assays
  • Innate immunity research
  • Wound healing studies
  • LPS neutralisation experiments
  • Membrane interaction and structural studies

Notes

LL-37 is strongly cationic and binds readily to plastic and glass. Use low-binding tubes, and note that serum components and divalent cations can reduce its antimicrobial activity in culture.

Storage and handling

Store the lyophilised powder at -20°C for up to one year, or at -80°C for one to two years. Warm the vial to room temperature before opening so moisture does not condense onto the powder. See our reconstitution procedure and handling and storage guides.
