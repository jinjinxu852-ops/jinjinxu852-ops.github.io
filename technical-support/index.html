Technical Support

Our chemists answer the questions that come before an order and the ones that come after it. There is no charge for advice on a sequence or a specification.

Before you order

Sequence feasibility review. Send a sequence and we will tell you how difficult it is to make, what purity is realistic and how long it will take. Long, hydrophobic or multiply bridged targets sometimes need a different strategy, and it is far better to know that before a purchase order exists.

Specification advice. Purity, quantity, salt form and modification choices all interact with each other and with your budget. Describe the experiment and we will propose a specification — including, where it applies, a cheaper one that would work just as well.

Modification selection. If you know you need a label but not which one, tell us the instrument and the assay. Dye choice should follow your filter sets, not a catalogue.

While your peptide is being made

If the sequence turns out to behave differently from expectation, we tell you rather than quietly delivering a lower purity. Where a change of approach is needed — a fragment strategy, a modified cleavage, a revised purity target — we explain the options and the cost implications before proceeding.

After delivery

Solubility problems. The most frequent support request, and usually solvable from the sequence alone. Start with our reconstitution procedure; if that does not work, send us the sequence and what you have tried.

Unexpected assay results. Before concluding that a peptide is inactive, it is worth ruling out the alternatives: insufficient dissolution, incorrect concentration because net peptide content was not used, residual TFA affecting cells, or degradation in storage. We can help work through them.

Verification of stored material. If a peptide has been in the freezer for a long time, an HPLC and MS check establishes whether it is still intact before you use it in an important experiment.

Repeat orders. Tell us the previous lot number and we will reproduce the same specification.

Peptides from other sources

We will look at material we did not make. Send the sequence, the supplier’s certificate of analysis and a description of the problem, and we will advise what analysis would settle the question. See peptide analysis services.

How to reach us

Email info@chempeptide.com with the sequence and your question, or use the contact form. Include as much detail as you can — sequence, modification, purity, how it was stored, what you observed. For project discussions, we are happy to arrange a call; we treat all sequences as confidential and can sign an NDA first.

Frequently asked questions

Is technical advice charged for?

No. Reviewing a sequence, recommending a purity grade or discussing how to approach a difficult target costs nothing and does not oblige you to order. Laboratory analysis of an existing sample is a paid service.

Can you tell me whether my sequence is feasible before I order?

Yes, and we would rather do that than take an order we cannot deliver well. Send the sequence and we will say what to expect in terms of purity, yield and time, and flag anything that could cause trouble.

My peptide will not dissolve. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the sequence and describe what you have tried. Solubility is usually predictable from the sequence, and a different solvent or pH solves most cases. See our reconstitution procedure for the general approach.

Will you look at a peptide from another supplier?

Yes. If a peptide is not behaving as expected, we can analyse it to establish what is actually in the vial. Send the sequence, the certificate of analysis and a description of the problem.
