About ChemPeptide

How it works

A controlled process behind every peptide

The same four steps apply whether you order a few milligrams or a multi-gram batch. See our quality and process control for details.

  1. 01

    Sequence review

    Our chemists check your sequence for difficult motifs and recommend the right purity, salt form and modifications before quoting.

  2. 02

    Solid-phase synthesis

    Fmoc SPPS with optimised coupling for long, hydrophobic and heavily modified sequences.

  3. 03

    HPLC purification

    Preparative reversed-phase HPLC to the agreed purity, followed by lyophilisation.

  4. 04

    QC and delivery

    Every peptide ships with its HPLC chromatogram and mass spectrum, so you can verify what is in the vial.
